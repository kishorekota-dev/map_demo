# Replace a lost, stolen or damaged card

Use this procedure when a customer asks for a replacement card. Agents must
confirm the replacement address before ordering a new card. Always tell the
customer that replacement cards arrive within 5 to 7 business days. Never say
"your card is already on its way" before the replacement has been ordered.

## Steps

1. Identify the card the customer wants to replace.
2. Ask why the card is being replaced: lost, stolen, damaged or expired.
3. If the card was lost or stolen, make sure it is blocked before ordering a replacement.
4. Confirm the replacement address on file with the customer.
5. Order the replacement card and give the customer the confirmation details.

## Guardrails

- Require confirmation
- Require fields: replacementAddress
- Escalate to a specialist when reason is stolen
- Never say: "no fee", "guaranteed delivery date"
- Disclose: Replacement cards arrive within 5 to 7 business days.
- Supervisors may waive the replacement fee for premium customers.
